## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Pure HTML5 / Vanilla CSS3 / Modern ES6+ JavaScript**: Zero external bloatware frameworks (no Tailwind, Bootstrap, or React runtime) for lightning-fast 60fps load times.
- **Epicura-Inspired Luxury Theme**: Deep royal emerald green (`#0d2b1e`), metallic gold (`#c9963a`), and warm cream ivory surfaces.
- **Responsive Across All Screens**:
  - **Laptops & MacBook Air**: Compact single-screen viewport fit.
  - **iPads & Tablets**: Balanced 2-column layout with sleek hamburger navigation.
  - **Foldable Phones & Mobile Devices**: Native single-card view with smooth swipe and next/prev arrow controls.
- **Interactive Catering Cost Estimator (₹)**: Live per-plate quote calculation in Indian Rupees based on guest count, menu tier, and live food counters.
- **Curated Menu Packages**: Pure Veg (Deluxe & Royal), Veg + Non-Veg (Royal Gold & Mughlai), and Grand Platinum Wedding Feasts.
- **Direct Lead Conversion**: Floating Call & WhatsApp buttons (`+91 88288 14609`) with instant pre-filled booking inquiry form.
